Eight open decisions

Record Rack, the catalogue of a record shop. Everything waiting on you and nothing else. Each point says what is wrong, where that was established, and what the choices are, with my advice marked. Your choices are kept in this browser; at the bottom is a button to take them with you as text.

First, a correction

The previous page assumed 4,000 records. That was the number of titles, not the number of copies: there are 11,200. That difference hits A3 directly, so that point below uses the recounted figures.

3 decisions

What the customer sees

These three live in the screen where a customer views and reserves a record.

A1 · catalogue → status label

"Reserved" means two things

A record a customer is holding and a record the shop has set aside carry the same label. The seller cannot see which of the two it is, and so calls back the wrong customer.

Measured today · status=reserved is set in two places, in customer-reservation.ts and in internal-hold.ts, with no distinguishing field.

A2 · catalogue → view record

The sleeve is below the fold

Six text fields sit above the image. A customer scrolls past label, year and condition before seeing what the record looks like, while the sleeve is the reason they clicked.

Quoted · From the walkthrough of 3 September. Not measured again today; the screen may have changed since.

A3 · catalogue → button and confirmation

The copy assumes several copies

The button says "Reserve a copy" and the confirmation "One more is available". For 94 percent of the catalogue there is exactly one. For a one-off, "a copy" is an odd phrase.

Measured today · 11,200 records, 10,528 of them with stock 1. The copy has no singular variant.

2 decisions

Who may do what

Two points about the difference between looking and deciding.

B1 · settings → price list

Every employee can change the price list

There is an admin role, but it is only checked when deleting a customer. Prices, discounts and opening hours are open to anyone who can log in.

Quoted · From the review of 8 September. The role gate exists in one place.

B2 · stock → write off

Nothing records who wrote off a record

Writing off removes the record from the catalogue and stores nothing except the new stock level. When the till and the catalogue disagree, there is no trail back.

Quoted · From the review of 8 September.

2 decisions

Keeping and promising

Two places where the app promises something or leaves something lying.

C1 · reservations

Reservations never expire

A reservation stays until someone removes it by hand. The oldest open reservation dates from 14 March and has since held a record that was requested three times.

Measured today · 61 open reservations, 23 of them older than 60 days. No expiry period in the data model.

C2 · footer → Shipping

"Coming soon: shipping" has been there since March

A promise without a date, visible to every customer, for six months now. Two customers have e-mailed about it.

Quoted · From the walkthrough of 3 September.

1 decision

Where the tests do not reach

One point, and it is the reason the stock bug could happen.

D1 · src/db/*-repo.ts

The real database path is not tested

Every repo exists twice: a SQLite version and an in-memory version. The tests run against the second, and it cannot make the first one's mistake: it returns what went in, while the SQLite version maps columns to fields. That is where the stock bug of August came from.

Measured today · 140 repo tests, all 140 against the in-memory version. Zero against SQLite.
